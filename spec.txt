PROJECT OVERVIEW
tpt-actuarial is the product vertical for actuarial modeling and insurance
reserving. The insurance and reinsurance industry runs its reserve calculations,
pricing models, and regulatory capital requirements on R (lifecontingencies,
ChainLadder, reserving package), Excel VBA macros, and $500k/seat proprietary
software (SAS Prophet, Moody's RiskIntegrity, Willis Towers Watson Emblem).
These workloads are: CPU-bound batch jobs (running thousands of stochastic
scenarios overnight), compliance-sensitive (regulators review the methodology,
not just the numbers), and require bit-identical reproducibility across runs
and across systems (audit requirements for Solvency II and IFRS 17).

Rust solves all three directly: multi-threaded stochastic scenario runs that
finish in minutes instead of hours, deterministic output that satisfies audit
requirements, and a single compiled binary that deploys to actuarial workstations
without an R or Python installation. The regulatory forcing function is real and
immediate: IFRS 17 (effective January 2023) and Solvency II are forcing insurers
to rebuild their actuarial platforms to meet new disclosure requirements — they
are already spending the money, and the incumbent tools are widely viewed as
inadequate for the new standards.

This vertical has no predecessor in tpt-solutions — nothing overlaps it.

ECOSYSTEM SURVEY — license decisions
Checked before scoping the crate inventory:

  rslife — MIT. QUALIFIES.
    Basic life-contingency math: annuity present values, insurance present
    values, mortality table lookups. 4,646 downloads, released mid-2025.
    Qualifies on license. However: the implementation is thin (single-decrement
    life tables only, no select-and-ultimate, no multi-state models). Assess
    whether to wrap rslife for the basic life-table primitives and build on
    top, or absorb its functionality directly into tpt-actuarial-mortality.
    Decision deferred to the prior-art check — read the source before deciding.

  kestrel — license: needs-verification (0.0.1 stub with no documentation).
    Investigate and discard if it remains a stub. Do not block on it.

  polars — MIT. QUALIFIES.
    DataFrame library. Loss triangles are naturally represented as 2D arrays
    indexed by accident year and development year. polars qualifies and handles
    this representation well. Assess whether tpt-actuarial-triangle should
    depend on polars for its triangle storage or use ndarray (below) instead.

  ndarray — MIT OR Apache-2.0. QUALIFIES.
    N-dimensional arrays. Alternative to polars for triangle storage if a
    pure-numeric 2D array is preferable to a DataFrame. Lower-level than
    polars; better suited for the numeric core of chain ladder algorithms.
    Likely the right choice for tpt-actuarial-triangle; polars better suited
    for tpt-actuarial-pricing where tabular data with column names matters.

  statrs — MIT OR Apache-2.0. QUALIFIES. Already wrapped by tpt-math-stats.
    Distribution fitting for severity/frequency distributions. Consume via
    tpt-math-stats / tpt-math-prob-dist, not directly.

No serious crates for loss development triangles, stochastic reserving,
Solvency II cash-flow projection, or IFRS 17 CSM roll-forward exist on
crates.io. All domain-specific crates below are build-from-scratch.

CORE PRINCIPLES
This vertical is shaped like a financial-modeling platform: deterministic
calculations that must be auditable, reproducible, and traceable to their
inputs. Every calculation should be expressible as a pure function of typed
inputs → typed outputs with no hidden state. The event-sourcing and audit-trail
patterns from tpt-erp-rust apply here — audit before reimplementing.

CRATE INVENTORY

  tpt-actuarial-mortality
    Build (possibly wrap rslife as a foundation — see ECOSYSTEM SURVEY).
    Mortality tables and survival models: select-and-ultimate tables (two-
    dimensional: select age × duration since selection), aggregate tables
    (one-dimensional: attained age), fractional-age interpolation methods
    (UDD — Uniform Distribution of Deaths, constant force, Balducci),
    Gompertz–Makeham parametric mortality law fitting, improvement scales
    (projection of future mortality improvement, e.g. Scale MP-2021 for US
    pension), multi-state models (alive/disabled/dead, able/unable/dead for
    long-term care). Life-contingency functions: curtate/complete future
    lifetime, annuity present values (immediate, due, deferred, n-year certain
    and life), insurance present values (term, whole life, endowment). no_std+alloc.
    Depends on: tpt-math-prob-core (Distribution trait for mortality
    distributions, survival function interface), tpt-math-prob-dist
    (Gompertz, Makeham as parametric distributions), tpt-math-optimize-general
    (MLE parameter fitting for Gompertz–Makeham), tpt-math-units (ages in
    years, interest rates as dimensionless ratios — enforce at the type level
    to prevent confusing force-of-mortality with annual effective rate).

  tpt-actuarial-triangle
    Build. Loss development triangles: the core data structure of P&C
    (property/casualty) reserving. Accident-year × development-year matrix of
    cumulative or incremental paid/incurred losses. Development factor selection
    (volume-weighted, simple-average, selected LDF). Chain ladder method
    (deterministic ultimate loss projection from selected link ratios). Bornhuetter-
    Ferguson method (credibility-weighted blend of chain ladder and a priori
    expected losses). Cape Cod method (a priori derived from the data itself).
    Clark LDF and Cape Cod methods (maximum-likelihood with parametric growth
    curves). Tail factor selection and extrapolation. no_std+alloc.
    Depends on: tpt-eng-timeseries (accident year / calendar year / development
    period time-series indexing), tpt-math-prob-core (MLE for Clark method
    distribution fitting), tpt-math-optimize-general (MLE optimization for
    Clark growth curve parameters). External: ndarray (triangle matrix storage).

  tpt-actuarial-reserve
    Build. Stochastic reserving: Mack chain ladder (distribution-free mean and
    variance of ultimate losses, process and parameter variance decomposition),
    Over-Dispersed Poisson (ODP) bootstrap (resample Pearson residuals to
    generate predictive reserve distributions, England-Verrall method),
    Merz-Wüthrich one-year reserve risk (calendar-year CDR — Claims
    Development Result — for Solvency II SCR calculation). Reserve range
    outputs: point estimate, standard error, VaR at 75th/95th/99.5th
    percentile (Solvency II SCR = 99.5th percentile one-year reserve risk).
    std (random sampling for bootstrap).
    Depends on: tpt-actuarial-triangle (input data structure),
    tpt-math-prob-monte-carlo (ODP bootstrap resampling engine),
    tpt-math-prob-dist (overdispersed Poisson, log-normal for reserve
    distributions), tpt-math-stats (chi-squared residual diagnostics).

  tpt-actuarial-cashflow
    Build. Regulatory cash-flow projection for IFRS 17 and Solvency II.
    IFRS 17 Contractual Service Margin (CSM) roll-forward: fulfilment cash
    flows (best-estimate liability), risk adjustment (RA), CSM accretion,
    loss component tracking for onerous contracts. Solvency II Technical
    Provisions: best-estimate liability (BEL) as probability-weighted present
    value of future cash flows discounted on the EIOPA risk-free curve, risk
    margin (cost-of-capital method: 6% × SCR(t) projected forward). Discount
    curve construction: EIOPA Smith-Wilson extrapolation to the Ultimate Forward
    Rate (UFR), UK/US Treasury curve bootstrapping. std.
    Depends on: tpt-actuarial-mortality (mortality rates driving life insurance
    cash flows), tpt-actuarial-reserve (reserve inputs for non-life BEL),
    tpt-eng-timeseries (cash-flow projection vectors, discount factor time
    series), tpt-math-optimize-general (Smith-Wilson calibration, yield curve
    fitting), tpt-math-prob-monte-carlo (stochastic cash-flow scenario
    generation for Internal Model BEL).
    Open design call: IFRS 17 General Measurement Model (GMM), Variable Fee
    Approach (VFA for with-profits/unit-linked), and Premium Allocation
    Approach (PAA for short-duration contracts) are three distinct measurement
    methods. Start with GMM (most general) and flag VFA and PAA as v2 scope.

  tpt-actuarial-credibility
    Build. Credibility theory: Bühlmann credibility (EBCT Model 1 — pure
    experience rating), Bühlmann-Straub credibility (EBCT Model 2 — volume-
    weighted experience rating), greatest-accuracy credibility (Bayesian
    credibility with conjugate priors), limited fluctuation credibility (full/
    partial credibility based on sample size thresholds). Used in commercial
    lines pricing to blend class-level experience with manual rates. no_std+alloc.
    Depends on: tpt-math-prob-core (prior/posterior conjugate pairs),
    tpt-math-prob-bayes (Bayesian credibility update), tpt-math-stats
    (variance/covariance estimation for Bühlmann parameters).

  tpt-actuarial-pricing
    Build. Insurance pricing and experience rating: GLM (Generalized Linear
    Model) rating plan structure (multiplicative/additive rating factors,
    base rate + relativities), loss ratio method, pure premium method,
    indicated rate change calculation (loss development + trend + expense
    loading), multivariate classification relativities via GBM (Gradient
    Boosted Model) for personal lines. This is the pricing-actuarial workflow;
    the underlying regression machinery comes from tpt-math-stats. std.
    Depends on: tpt-actuarial-credibility (credibility-weighted experience),
    tpt-actuarial-triangle (loss development to ultimate for pricing loss
    ratios), tpt-math-stats (GLM fitting, regression diagnostics),
    tpt-math-prob-dist (frequency and severity distribution fitting).
    Open design call: whether GBM/ML-based relativities belong in this crate
    or in a separate tpt-actuarial-ml crate — defer until a real pricing
    consumer clarifies how much ML-based rating is needed vs. traditional GLM.

  tpt-actuarial (umbrella)
    Re-exports mortality + triangle + reserve + cashflow + credibility +
    pricing behind Cargo features.
    Depends on: all six crates above, each behind a named Cargo feature.

NOT IN SCOPE HERE
Insurance product administration (policy administration, premium billing,
claims workflow, policy management) — these are closer to tpt-erp-rust's
event-sourced ledger shape than an actuarial modeling tool. Policy admin is
a separate vertical (or tpt-erp-rust itself) — not here. Core probability
distributions and statistical estimation belong in tpt-math-prob-core and
tpt-math-stats respectively; if tpt-actuarial-mortality surfaces a distribution
(e.g., Makeham) that other verticals would use, route it upstream.
